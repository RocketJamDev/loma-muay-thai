Hosted by Thailand insiders — with over 7 years living in Phuket, we'll help you experience Thailand the way most visitors never do.

01 — Muay Thai Training

All levels welcome.

Train twice a day in a supportive and local environment. Every session is adapted to your level, whether you're new to Muay Thai or already have experience. We make sure you get the guidance and attention you need to improve throughout the trip — you won't feel like just another number.

02 — Stay in Paradise

Phuket resort.

Wake up in a beautiful tropical resort just minutes from the gym. Relax by the pool, recharge between sessions, and enjoy the perfect balance of training and recovery.

03 — Discover Real Phuket

Explore beautiful beaches.

Go beyond the tourist hotspots and experience the Phuket most visitors never see. Having lived in Thailand for over seven years, we'll take you to our favorite beaches, viewpoints, cafés and hidden gems.

04 — Meet Your Coach

Paloma Arranz.

Every session on camp is personally overseen by Paloma, a Spanish professional Muay Thai and kickboxing athlete living and fighting out of Phuket. Training under Leo Elias at Phuket Fight Club, she's won two Muay Thai world titles and is currently ranked #4 in the world by the WBC.
